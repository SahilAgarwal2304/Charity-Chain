Project Viva & Defense Guide

System Architecture & Technical Viva Q&A

Everything you need to confidently answer external examiners during practical oral examination.

Traditional Charity (Problems)
  • Opaque Ledger: Donors have no visibility into how funds are actually spent.
  • High Middlemen Fees: Banks & payment aggregators take 3% to 10% cut.
  • Central Point of Failure: Centralized databases can be altered, wiped, or fabricated.
  • Misappropriation: Founders can redirect donations without donor knowledge.
CharityChain Solution (Our Project)
  • 100% Immutability: Every donation is cryptographically sealed in blocks.
  • Direct Peer-to-Peer: Smart contracts eliminate administrative middlemen.
  • Automated Escrow: Funds locked in Solidity contract until rules are satisfied.
  • Zero Intermediary Trust: Code is law — neither creator nor admin can steal funds.

Quick Viva Cheat Sheet (Examiner FAQs)

1. Why use Blockchain? Why not a standard SQL/NoSQL database?

Answer: In a traditional database (MySQL/PostgreSQL), the database administrator (DBA) or server owner has root access and can easily execute UPDATE or DELETE queries to manipulate donation amounts or falsify transactions. On Ethereum blockchain, once a transaction is mined, it is permanently immutable. Smart contracts guarantee that funds can only be released to designated beneficiaries.

2. What is a Smart Contract and how does it work here?

Answer: A smart contract is self-executing code stored on the blockchain at a specific cryptographic address. In CharityChain, our Solidity contract (`CharityPlatform.sol`) acts as an automated escrow: it tracks campaigns, receives Ether via the `donate()` function, updates balances, and releases funds via `withdrawFunds()` strictly according to programmed rules.

3. What is Gas and Gas Limit?

Answer: Gas is the execution fee required to perform a computational operation on the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM). Gas Limit is the maximum amount of gas the sender is willing to spend on a transaction. It prevents infinite loops in smart contract execution.

4. How does MetaMask fit into this architecture?

Answer: MetaMask acts as a non-custodial crypto wallet and Web3 provider. It injects the window.ethereum object into the browser, securely signs transactions using the user's private key, and broadcasts them to the Ethereum node via JSON-RPC.
